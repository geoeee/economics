Economics / 经济学

An interdisciplinary learning project exploring economics through the lenses of history, politics, finance, mathematics, and psychology.

How To Use / 使用方式

Navigate the timeline chronologically from ancient economies to the present day. Each period page covers six disciplinary perspectives. Concept pages provide deep dives into fundamental ideas, and Cross-Discipline pages synthesize knowledge across fields.

Timeline Overview / 时间线总览

3500 BC – 500 AD
Ancient / 古代
Barter, coinage, Mesopotamia, Egypt — the origins of money and trade.
500 BC – 500 AD
Classical / 古典时代
Greek city-states, Roman Republic & Empire — taxation, currency, and early economic thought.
500 – 1500
Medieval / 中世纪
Feudalism, Silk Road, Islamic Golden Age — land as wealth, long-distance trade.
1500 – 1760
Mercantilism / 重商主义
Colonial empires, joint-stock companies, bullionism — the birth of modern trade.
1760 – 1870
Industrial Revolution / 工业革命
Adam Smith, Marx, factories, steam — capitalism takes shape.
1870 – 1914
Golden Age / 黄金时代
Gold standard, first globalization, marginal revolution — the belle époque of capitalism.
1914 – 1945
World Wars & Depression / 战争与萧条
WWI, Great Depression, WWII — collapse of the old order, rise of Keynes.
1945 – 1971
Bretton Woods / 战后秩序
IMF, World Bank, fixed exchange rates — rebuilding global capitalism.
1971 – 2008
Neoliberal Era / 新自由主义
Fiat money, financialization, Washington Consensus — free markets ascendant.
2008 – Present
Contemporary / 当代
GFC, crypto, deglobalization, pandemic — uncertainty and transformation.
